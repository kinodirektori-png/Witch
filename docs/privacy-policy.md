Medea is a game for children about the real chemistry in a home kitchen. We built it so that a child can play
without anything about them leaving the device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Medea does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information or usage data.

- No accounts, sign-in, names, ages, email addresses or photos.
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No location, camera, microphone or contacts.
- No chat, social features, or in-app purchases.
- The game does not use the internet while you play.

## What stays on the device

To remember progress, Medea saves a small file on the device itself: which recipes have been brewed, which book pages
are unlocked, how many of each kitchen ingredient are left, and the current recipe. This file never leaves the device,
contains no personal information, and is deleted when the app is deleted.

## Links outside the game

If the game ever shows a link to a website (for example, this policy), a grown-up question appears first so that
a child can't leave the game on their own.

## Children's privacy (COPPA and GDPR-K)

Medea is designed for children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, no parental consent is needed
to play. If you believe information about a child has reached us in any way, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date. We will not start
collecting data without updating this policy and asking for verifiable parental consent first, where the law
requires it.
